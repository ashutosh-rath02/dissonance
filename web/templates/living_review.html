What is this?

AI researchers publish hundreds of papers evaluating language models -- claims like "model X scores 84% on benchmark Y" or "chain-of-thought prompting improves reasoning by 15%." Different papers sometimes quietly contradict each other, but nobody notices because the papers never cite one another or use different wording.

Dissonance reads papers automatically, extracts each factual claim as structured data (who/what/how much/under what conditions), finds pairs of claims that are about the same thing, and has an AI adjudicator carefully compare each pair: genuine contradiction, or just a difference in scope or setup that only looks like one? Every verdict below links back to the exact sentence it came from in each paper, re-verified live on every page load.
